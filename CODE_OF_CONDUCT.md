# Academic Code of Conduct & Research Ethics

## 1. Academic Purpose & Integrity

This repository represents the official software engineering and academic portfolio of undergraduate studies in **Economic Informatics** at the **Faculty of Economics and Business Administration (FEAA)**, **University of Craiova**.

Contributors, maintainers, and researchers engaging with this monorepo pledge to uphold the highest standards of scientific rigor, intellectual honesty, and ethical engineering:

1. **Plagiarism & Authorship:** All academic text, analytical formulations, and software implementations are either the original work of the author or explicitly attributed to upstream sources, scientific publications, and open-source projects via formal academic citations.
2. **Deterministic Reproducibility:** No simulation results, attack telemetry, or test reports may be fabricated or artificially manipulated. Every metric reported in academic theses must be reproducible via the automated toolchain.
3. **Respectful Collaboration:** We foster an inclusive, collegial, and constructively critical learning and research environment. Harassment, condescension, or discriminatory behavior in issues, discussions, or code reviews will not be tolerated.

## 2. Responsible Cybersecurity Research

Projects within this repository (notably `licenta/LL3-LucrareLicenta`) include attack and defense simulation tools based on the MITRE ATT&CK framework:
* These tools are developed exclusively for educational purposes, defensive validation, and cyber resilience evaluation in virtualized, isolated sandbox environments.
* Weaponization of this software or unauthorized deployment against third-party production infrastructure is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Enforcement

Instances of academic dishonesty, ethical violations, or abusive behavior should be reported directly to the repository maintainer (@stefanutc1) or the FEAA Academic Integrity Board.
